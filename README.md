A **Portfolio** is a personal or professional showcase that highlights your skills, projects, experiences, and achievements in a structured and visually appealing way. 
It typically includes sections like **About Me, Education, Skills, Projects, Certifications, and Contact Information**, allowing visitors or potential employers to 
quickly understand your capabilities. Built using technologies like **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**,
a portfolio can feature interactive elements, animations, and responsive design to enhance user experience. Beyond just a collection of work, 
a portfolio demonstrates your **creativity, technical expertise, and professional growth**, serving as a dynamic tool to impress recruiters or clients and strengthen your personal brand.
